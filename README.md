# AI Native Design for Product Designers — Course Handout

Teaching material for product designers who want to use AI professionally across the design process. The course follows one case study, QuickBook, a booking product for a beauty salon with three branches.

## What is in this repository

- **Full Course Handout.pdf**: the handouts of Sessions 1 to 5, with the skill files and templates in the appendix.
- **Prompts.md**: only the prompts, with their names, in the order used in class, so you can copy them.

## Sessions

1. Understanding AI
2. From Brief to Evidence
3. From Evidence to Wireframe
4. Design to Technical Understanding
5. Prototype
